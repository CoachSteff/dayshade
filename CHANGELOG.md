# Changelog

## 1.1.0 · 27 November 2026

- Settings window with Colours, Logos, Bars and General panes, and a live preview of your main screen.
- Colour schemes are a list: edit the seven built-ins (and reset them), add your own, import and export them as JSON.
- Motion, vibrance, dark mode depth and grain sliders, with a percentage bubble while you drag.
- Location: use your Mac's location or search for any place in the world.
- Logos: add your own SVG, PDF or PNG marks, with colour, opacity, shadow and display options.
- Bars: glassy strips under the menu bar, shelves behind the Dock, and custom zones.
- Placement grid: drag zones to move them, drag the corner to resize; colour shows the selection and the pointer shows what a drag will do.
- Open at login, menu bar icon toggle, frame rate choice, frame export, optional system wallpaper sync.
- About panel with version, author and licence. Released under the GNU GPL v3 or later.

## 1.0.0 · 27 November 2026

- Live mesh gradient wallpaper with The House of Coaching logo, colours following the sun and light or dark mode.
